# A Complete Guide to Simple Arrays

## ${symbol.gyromancy.fire}

Fireball: with ${symbol.gyromancy.fire} as its only rune, the array creates a fireball that explodes on contact and continuously consumes mana from the air.

- Variants:
  - Stabilized: add ${symbol.gyromancy.fix} as the sole modifier. The fireball no longer explodes and can be used for smelting and cooking. It is still extremely hot.

## ${symbol.gyromancy.water}

Water Orb: with ${symbol.gyromancy.water} as its only rune, the array creates an orb of water that can carry items and dissolve potions. It bursts on impact and continuously consumes mana from the air.

- Variants:
  - Stabilized: add ${symbol.gyromancy.fix} as the sole modifier. The orb no longer bursts and can be used for brewing.

## ${symbol.gyromancy.star}

Illumination: with ${symbol.gyromancy.star} as its only rune, the array creates a ball of light that follows automatically.

Projection: use ${symbol.gyromancy.star} + ${symbol.gyromancy.split} to project the remaining structure. Its distance depends on the arrows, and its size matches the array. **The projection can resonate with mana.**

## ${symbol.gyromancy.mana}

Mana Bolt: with ${symbol.gyromancy.mana} as its only rune, the array creates a bolt of mana that draws in the mana around it.
