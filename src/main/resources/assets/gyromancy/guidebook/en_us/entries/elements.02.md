# Element Crystals

## Condensed Elements

Where the element concentration is high enough, an element effect begins to grow the matching element crystal in the empty space it covers.

Fire, water and wind projectiles can all do this.

# How Crystals Persist

A crystal is sustained by the concentration around it. Once that concentration falls below what it needs, the crystal starts to crack apart; when it finally shatters it drops 1-3 of the matching element crystals.

Fire, water and wind crystals glow faintly on their own, and a light crystal shines like a lamp; earth and dark crystals give off no light.

Nobody has managed to cultivate an earth, light or dark crystal yet.
