# Using a Wand

Shift + right-click the wand to open its configuration, then place a canvas into a projection slot. Hold ordinary right-click to unfold a projection before you; release it, and the projection ceases. Some wands can superimpose several canvas layers, perhaps allowing canvases of different sizes to form more complex patterns. Unfortunately, the nature of optical projection arrays means that traces unable to resonate with mana cannot be captured. Owing to a peculiar law of resonance, the wand appears able to distinguish valid arrays naturally: only arrays capable of activation are projected.
