# Runes: How Spells Act

## Optional Runes

Beyond the runes that form the basis of an array, there are often optional modifier runes:

:::image gyromancy:textures/guide/example.png width=60 height=60
:::

Arrows grant additional direction and speed.

Other parameters alter rotation, reversal, fixation, splitting, or repetition. They cannot be piled on at will: only parameters accepted by the central spell carry meaning. If the current spell cannot accept an extra parameter, the entire array remains silent.
