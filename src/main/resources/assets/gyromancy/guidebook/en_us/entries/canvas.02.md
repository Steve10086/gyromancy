# Basic Controls

## Simple to Use?

Placing a stored scroll does not activate its arrays. Right-click the scroll to open the editor; when you leave the editor, the canvas unfurls and activates valid arrays. Shift-place to unfurl immediately, or Shift-right-click a stored scroll to unfurl it directly. You will also need a pen and ink.

# Placement Direction

The canvas is anchored at the bottom-left corner of your view: its bottom edge always faces you, and it grows towards your right and away from you, so placing from a different direction occupies different blocks. Wall canvases stay upright; floor and ceiling canvases rotate with your facing (a ceiling canvas looks upside down when you look up at it from the side you placed it).

Blank canvases of the same size stack; once anything is drawn they no longer stack. In creative mode, middle-click a placed canvas to get a complete copy that carries its recognized rune structure.

# The Editor

Select the tool you wish to use from the hotbar.

Use Ctrl + scroll and drag to control the view's scale and position.

The panel on the right can help you draw more complex arrays: besides undo, redo, clear and resolution controls it holds a row of rotate and mirror buttons (counter-clockwise, clockwise, mirror horizontally, mirror vertically). The whole canvas is transformed at once, and every transform can be undone or redone.

Ctrl + Z undoes and Ctrl + R redoes (rebind them under Options - Controls - Gyromancy; they only apply inside the canvas and carving screens).
