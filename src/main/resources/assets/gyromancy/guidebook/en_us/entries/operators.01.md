# The Principles Beneath Mana

## On the Relation Between Array Effects and Non-Elemental Runes

The solution of a magic array has long been thought to obey no law whatsoever, determined entirely by the resonance between rune and mana. After long study and collation, I shall overturn those former theorems in this paper and establish a formula for the construction of arrays that accords with human understanding.

<!-- page -->

Most array effects share the same basis. Adding an arrow to a fireball array, for example, does not alter the fireball's form; it grants direction on top of that form. Traditional thought has always held such modifications to be irregular and impossible to generalize, but I have discovered independent functional clusters. Every rune within a cluster can be accepted by any base rune that accepts that cluster. This, beyond doubt, allows us to deliberately construct arrays that have never existed before..
