# Modifier Arrays and Their Scope

## Motion

With ${symbol.gyromancy.motion} as its sole rune, this modifier can be applied to nearly every array, granting speed and acceleration to its effect. A few arrays respond differently.

It accepts ${symbol.gyromancy.arrow}-type symbols, adds them together, and applies the result to the effect.

It also accepts another Motion array as a modifier, treating its result as acceleration.
