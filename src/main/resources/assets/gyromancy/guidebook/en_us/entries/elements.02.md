# Element Crystals

## Condensed Elements

Where the element concentration is high enough, an element effect begins to grow the matching element crystal in the empty space it covers.

Fire, water and wind projectiles can all do this.

# How Crystals Persist

A crystal is sustained by the concentration around it. Once that concentration falls below what it needs, the crystal shatters and drops 1-3 of the matching element crystals.

Nobody has managed to cultivate an earth crystal yet.
